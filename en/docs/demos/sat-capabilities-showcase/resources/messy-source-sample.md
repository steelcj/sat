1. Introduction

The Care and Feeding of Digital Archives

Every organization keeps things — reports, guides,
photographs, the odd spreadsheet nobody dares delete —
and almost none of it carries the one thing that makes it
trustworthy later: a record of what it is and where it
came from.

This short note — written quickly, never catalogued —
is exactly the kind of document that piles up. It has no
title metadata, no stated author, no license, and no way
to prove it hasn't been quietly edited since.

2. Why it matters

* Provenance is cheap to capture at creation and
  expensive to reconstruct afterward.
- Integrity you cannot demonstrate is integrity you do
  not really have.
+ Licensing decided years later is licensing decided
  under pressure.

---

2. What good looks like

A well-kept archive answers four questions without
hesitation — who, when, under what terms, and unaltered
since when. That is the whole game.
