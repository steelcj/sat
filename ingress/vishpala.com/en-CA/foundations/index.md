*A demo section. It shows what this template delivers by holding this very site to the same measure we use to evaluate any product or service — the Universal Cake Evaluation Metrics. What follows are properties you can verify, not promises.*

## Accessible by default

Accessibility is built in, not bolted on. Every page in every site ships with real landmarks and headings, a skip link, keyboard operability, and an Infusion bar for text size, contrast, and spacing. Any motion respects your system's reduced-motion setting. While we use specification such as WCAG 2.1 AA to measure compliance we strive to make our work as accessible to as many individuals as possible.

[Read more about how accessibility plays out in the real world](./accessibility.md)

## Fast and light on bandwidth

The site is static HTML with self-hosted fonts and almost no JavaScript. There is no framework to download before the page appears. On a slow connection or an old phone, it still loads — because there is very little to load.

[Read more about what keeps pages fast and light](./performance.md)

## Energy-conserving

Small pages served from static hosting draw less power to build, to send, and to render. No heavy trackers run in the background, no analytics pipeline warms up on every visit. Efficiency here is an environmental choice as much as a performance one.

[Read more about the energy a website spends](./energy.md)

## Fully multilingual

Each language is a first-class, independent tree, not an afterthought grafted onto an English original. Translations are paired by a stable identity, so the language switcher always lands on the right counterpart — and when a translation is missing, the site says so honestly rather than dumping you on a wrong page.

[Read more about how the multilingual model works](./multilingual.md)

## Portable content

Your content is plain Markdown with open, standard metadata (Dublin Core). It is not trapped in a proprietary database or a page builder. If you leave this platform, you take your content with you, readable by any tool — and it keeps working even if the tools that authored it go away.

[Read more about what portable content means for you](./portable-content.md)

## Discoverable by search

Semantic HTML, clean URLs, a sitemap, canonical links, and per-language `hreflang` are part of the build, not a plugin you configure later. Search engines can read the structure because the structure is real.

[Read more about the SEO that is built in](./seo.md)

## Resilient — no third-party outage

Fonts, scripts, and assets are served from your own site, not borrowed from someone else's CDN. There is no external service that can go down and take your typography, your layout, or your forms with it.

[Read more about staying up when others go down](./resilient.md)

## Yours to keep

The template is licensed under the AGPL, and you own the repository and the data. There is no lock-in and no ransom for an export: leaving is a `git clone` away. The measure that matters is reversibility — the cost to you of walking away — and here it is close to zero.

[Read more about what owning your site really means](./ownership.md)
