The most important question to ask of any tool is not what it does while you stay, but what it costs you to leave. On this site, that cost is close to zero — by design.

## You own the code

The template is licensed under the AGPL, a licence that guarantees you can use, study, modify, and share it. It is not a rental with terms that can be changed under you. The site's code is yours to keep and to adapt, and it stays that way.

## You own the content and the data

Your pages live in a repository you control, in open formats you can read. There is no vendor holding your content hostage behind an export fee, a paywall, or a proprietary format that only their product can open. What visitors send you through the site is yours as well, not harvested into someone else's data business.

## Leaving is a `git clone` away

Because the code is open and the content is portable, moving away is a copy, not a migration project. You can take the whole site — code, content, and history — and host it elsewhere, hand it to another team, or fork it in a new direction. Nothing has to be negotiated or unlocked first.

## Reversibility is the measure

The Universal Cake Evaluation Metrics ask a single sharp question about power: how reversible is the relationship, and what does the weaker party pay to walk away? This is the heart of the *Agency* pillar. Here, we have tried to make the answer "almost nothing" — which is the strongest claim a tool can honestly make about respecting the people who use it.

This is one of our [foundations](/en-ca/foundations/).
