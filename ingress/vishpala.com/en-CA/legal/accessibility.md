## Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use assistive technologies. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## What we have done

The site is built with real headings and landmarks, works with the keyboard, respects reduced-motion preferences, and offers text-size and contrast adjustments through the accessibility bar.

## Known limitations

Some third-party content may not yet meet our standard. We are working to identify and address these areas.

## Contact us

If you encounter a barrier, or need information in a different format, please tell us through the [contact page](/en-ca/contact/). We will do our best to help and to fix the problem.
