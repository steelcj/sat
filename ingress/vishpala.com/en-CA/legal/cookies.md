## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a site stores on your device. They let a site remember your actions and preferences over time.

## How we use them

This site uses only what it needs to function and to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used:

- Essential cookies that keep the site working, such as remembering your language and accessibility settings.
- Optional measurement that helps us see which pages are useful, only if you allow it.

## Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
